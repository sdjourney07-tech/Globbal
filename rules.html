Rules

Overview

Globbal is a geography word game for two players. You score points by placing letter tiles on the board to spell place names from the Globbal dictionary — cities, countries, rivers, islands, kingdoms, and more. Highest score when the game ends wins.

The board

  • The board is 15 columns wide and 19 rows tall.
  • The center square is marked START. It also counts as a double-word (DW) square.
  • Premium squares multiply your score:
    • DL — double letter
    • TL — triple letter
    • DW — double word
    • TW — triple word
    • QW — quadruple word (Globbal only)
  • A premium bonus applies only to tiles you place on that turn. Tiles already on the board never re-trigger their square.

Tiles and rack

  • The tile bag holds 148 tiles, including 6 blanks.
  • Each player keeps a rack of 16 tiles.
  • Letter values follow standard Scrabble scoring. Blanks are worth 0 points.
  • When you play a blank, choose any letter A–Z. It stays that letter once placed.
  • After a successful turn, your rack refills from the bag.

Your turn

  • Place tiles from your rack onto empty squares, then tap Submit Turn.
  • Use Recall to take back tiles you have not yet submitted.
  • Shuffle reorders the tiles on your rack.
  • Pass Turn ends your turn without scoring. Pending tiles return to your rack and you do not draw new tiles.
  • There is no tile-swap or exchange action.

Placing words

  • Every turn, all new tiles must lie in one row or one column, with no gaps.
  • The first move must cover the START square.
  • Later moves must connect to tiles already on the board.
  • Every horizontal or vertical line of two or more letters formed or extended by your move must be a legal word.
  • Single letters by themselves do not count as words.

Legal words

Only names in the Globbal dictionary are legal. Browse the dictionary from the sign-in page to see countries, cities, rivers, seas, islands, historical kingdoms, and related place names.

  • Words must be at least 2 letters long.
  • Words with digits are not playable — the tile bag has no number tiles.
  • On the board, names are spelled without spaces. For example, play NEWYORK, not separate tiles for “NEW” and “YORK”.
  • Accents are ignored — SAOPAULO matches São Paulo.
  • Many compound geographic names also have shorter legal forms:
    • Full compact form: CRYSTALLAKE, NILERIVER, LAKESUPERIOR
    • Distinctive part: CRYSTAL, NILE, SUPERIOR
    • “… of …” names: BAYOFBENGAL or BENGAL
  • The generic feature word alone is not enough — you cannot play just LAKE, RIVER, ISLAND, or similar by stripping a compound name.

Scoring

  • For each word formed on your turn, add up the letter values of its tiles.
  • Apply letter premiums (DL, TL) and word premiums (DW, TW, QW) only on squares where you placed a tile this turn.
  • If a word crosses several word premiums, those multipliers multiply together.
  • Score every valid word formed in the turn, then add them together.
  • Play all 16 tiles in one turn for a 114-point bonus.
  • Words that use a new tile on a QW square are highlighted in gold on the board.

Invalid moves

Moves are checked automatically when you submit. There is no separate word-challenge step.

  • If any formed word is illegal, the move is rejected and your pending tiles return to your rack.
  • Other common rejections: tiles not in a straight line, gaps in a word, failing to connect to existing tiles, or leaving a blank unassigned.

End of game

The game ends when the tile bag is empty and a player submits a turn that clears their rack. The player with the higher score wins. Ties are possible in online matches.

Online and practice

  • Online games are turn-based between two signed-in players. You only see your opponent’s tiles after they submit.
  • Practice uses the same rules on one device, with two players taking turns locally.
